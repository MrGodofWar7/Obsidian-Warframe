# Abilities

## Passive
Allies in Affinity Range gain 50% of Trinity's Max Energy as Health.
## Well of Life (1)

> [!QUOTE] In-game Tooltip
> Create a well of life on an enemy who will absorb Status Effect damage intended for nearby allies. Allies gain additional Health when they attack the target. If allies die, enemies in the well of life die in their stead.

`Immortality` refers to the event where an ally dies while in the range of the Well of Life. It has a cooldown of 60s and invulnerability duration of 5s.
