# Dera Vandal Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire high power beams and gain Magnetic damage. Reduces Fire Rate but gains 3m Punch Through.

### Evolution `II`

Honestly... both can be good.
#### Paragon Essence (2)
- Increase Base Damage by +4
- On Status Effect: +5% Fire Rate for 5s. Stacks up to 6x.

#### Crimson Overture (1)
- Increase Base Damage by +6
- On Kill: +20% Ammo Efficiency for 5s. Stacks up to 4x.

### Evolution `III`: Swift Deliverance (1)
+50% Projectile Speed

### Evolution `IV`

Both can be used depending on what you want.

#### High Ground (2)

Increase Base Critical Chance by +25% of Current Status Chance, up to 35%.
#### Deathtrap Trigger (1)

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +14%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.4x
