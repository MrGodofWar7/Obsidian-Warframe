# Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Sacrifice silence for increased projectile size and Heat Damage. Infinite body Punch Through.

### Evolution `II`

#### Hitman's Opportunity (1)

This is mainly used as you're most likely not going to be using all 3 of Stalker's weapons at the same time.

- Increase Base Damage by +70
- +100% Damage to enemies below half Health.

#### Stalker's Resentment (2)

This requires you to not miss anything to maintain the buff.

- Increase Base Damage by +50
- With Hate and Despair equipped:
    - On hit:
    - Increase Base Damage by +10
    - +10% Fire Rate
    - Stacks up to 5x.
    - **Resets on missed shot**.

### Evolution `III`: Swift Deliverance (1)

- +30% Projectile Speed

Why not the other nodes? Bows are very ammo efficient, and if you want less zoom you can just... hip fire? Having faster projectile speed on a slow projectile will help you not miss at long distances.

### Evolution `IV`: Zeroed In

- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +1x.
