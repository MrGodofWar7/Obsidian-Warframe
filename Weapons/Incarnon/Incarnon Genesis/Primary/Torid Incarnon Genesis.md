# Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire a long-range Toxin beam that chains between enemies.
### Evolution `II`: Final Fusillade

- Increase Base Damage by +51
- +3 Multishot on last shot in magazine

### Evolution `III`: Renewed Horror

Reload from empty: Lingering damage field duration doubles on first shot.

### Evolution `IV`

#### (2) Survivor's Edge 

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +15%
- Increase Base Status Chance by +15%

#### (1) Commodore's Fortune

Increase Base Critical Chance by +20%
