# Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire homing and bouncing Heat damage explosive discs.

### Evolution `II`: Swift Sawblades

Increase Base Damage by +77
With Channeled Ability active: +70% Fire Rate

### Evolution `III`: Ready Retaliation

On Reload from empty: +100% Reload Speed

> [!INFO]
> Increased Projectile Speed will result in tracking issues in Incarnon form, and this weapon is very ammo efficient already so the additional base ammo capacity is really not needed.

### Evolution `IV`: Commodore's Fortune

Increase Base Critical Chance by +22%

> [!INFO]
> The other nodes are practically speaking... not needed.
