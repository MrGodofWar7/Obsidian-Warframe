# Dual Toxocyst Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Gain an Auto Fire mode and Ricochet

### Evolution `II`

Either [[Status Overload]] or +2 Multishot. Your choice.

#### Carnage Reign (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +60
- +33% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target

#### Fevered Frenzy (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +50
- On Ability Cast: +5% Multishot. Stacks up to 20x.

### Evolution `III`: Marksman's Hand

> [!INFO]
> In Normal form, Dual Toxocyst has high recoil before landing a Headshot hit.

-50% Weapon Recoil

### Evolution `IV`

Either more Critical Chance or additional Toxin damage.

#### Commodore's Fortune (1)

Increase Base Critical Chance by +20%

#### Neurotoxin (2)

On Headshot: +70% Toxin for 3s
