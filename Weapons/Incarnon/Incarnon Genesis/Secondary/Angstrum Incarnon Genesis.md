# Prisma Angstrum Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Launch target-seeking fireballs that ricochet towards enemies

### Evolution `II`

#### Haven Foray (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +50
- With Overshields: Increase Base Damage by +50

#### Paladin Virtue (2)

> [!WARNING]
> You should only use this if you can meet these requirements on your Warframe either with a naturally high base Energy Max in combination with the use of [[Arcane Battery]] and / or [[Primed Flow]].

- Increase Base Damage by +75
- With Energy Max over 700: Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.5x.

### Evolution `III`: Swift Deliverance

+30% Projectile Speed

### Evolution `IV`: Fatal Affliction

+40% Direct Damage per Status Type affecting the target

## Encumber

> [!WARNING]
> This weapon has very high recoil to the point that it's not easy to aim with in its Incarnon Form. It is mandatory to use recoil reduction mods so you can land headshots consistently without fighting recoil similar to that of headbanging while using a silly spray can with no control.
