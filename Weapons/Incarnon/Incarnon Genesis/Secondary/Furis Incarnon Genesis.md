# Furis Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire a short range plasma blast and gain Heat damage.

### Evolution `II`: Stormburst

- Increase Base Damage by +28
- On hitting enemies affected by Electricity Status:
    - +40% Multishot for 2s. Stacks up to 3x

### Evolution `III`: Practiced Grip

Increase Accuracy by +50%

### Evolution `IV`: Elemental Balance

Increase Base Status Chance by +28%

> [!WARNING]
> I know Headcracker (1) may look appealing for the +50% Fire Rate for 2s, but that also causes the Incarnon Form to fire even faster resulting in less ammo overall if you're still shooting at an enemy that is already dead.
