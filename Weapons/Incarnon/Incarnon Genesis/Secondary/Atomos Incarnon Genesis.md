# Atomos Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire high-powered grenades

### Evolution `II`

#### Hoplite Virtue (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +24
- On Shield Break: Increase Base Damage by +30 for 8s

#### Paladin Virtue (1)

Only use this if you can meet the Energy Max requirements.
- Increase Base Damage by +24
- With Energy Max over 700: Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +1x

### Evolution `III`: Mercenary Chamber

Increase Base Ammo Capacity to 560

### Evolution `IV`: Commodore's Fortune

Increase Base Critical Chance by +19%
