# Lex Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Fire energy waves that deal Radiation damage and gain Punch Through.

### Evolution `II`: Hoplite Virtue

> [!INFO] Why not Trusty Sidearm (2)?
> Lex Prime is very ammo efficient.

- Increase Base Damage by +20
- On Shield Break: Increase Base Damage by +80 for 8s

### Evolution `III`: Lex Talionis

On Headshot: -20% Weapon Recoil and +20% Accuracy for 4s. Stacks up to 4x.

### Evolution `IV`

You can choose between having more Critical Chance and Critical Damage with Critical Parallel (3) or if you're a quick Headshot person go with Executioner's Dawn (1)

#### Critical Parallel (3)

- Increase Base Critical Chance by +19%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +0.4x

#### Executioner's Dawn (1)

On Equip: +100% Headshot Damage for 4s
