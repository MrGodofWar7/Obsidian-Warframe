# Kunai Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Incarnon `I`: Incarnon Form

Projectiles seek Headshots and have increased Multishot

### Incarnon `II`

Swift Conclusion is preferred.
#### Swift Conclusion (1)

- Increase Base Damage by +70
- +200% Damage to enemies below half Health.

#### Sage's Resolve (2)

- Increase Base Damage by +80
- With Channeled Ability active: +100% Multishot


### Incarnon `III`: Swift Deliverance

> [!INFO]
> Increased Projectile Speed is preferred as Projectile Speed in both forms is quite slow.

+50% Projectile Speed

### Incarnon `IV`: Deathtrap Trigger

On Equip from Primary:
- Increase Base Critical Chance by +40%
- Increase Base Critical Damage Multiplier by +1.4x for 4s
