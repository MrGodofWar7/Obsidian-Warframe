# Bronco Prime Builds

## Incarnon Evolutions

> [!NOTE]
> Weak Point hits charge Incarnon Transmutation; Alt Fire transmutes. Switching back will expend any remaining charge.

These are the Evolutions used with all of the builds below. If there are multiple evolutions picked in the same node, they are ordered in priority from first to last.

### Evolution `I`: Incarnon Form

Increase Range and Ricochet.

### Evolution `II`: Infused Shots

> [!WARNING]
> See Projectile Speed would be nice, but it's not really necessary when all it does is extend the damage falloff on a hitscan weapon.


- Increase Base Damage by +16
- On 50 Energy spent: Increase Base Damage by ++9 for 10s. Stacks up to 4x.

### Evolution `III`: Practiced Grip

> [!WARNING]
> The thing about Bronco Prime is the bullet spread is very spread out. The only way to fix this is with an Accuracy increase.

Increase Accuracy by +30%

### Evolution `IV`: Commodore's Fortune

> [!FAIL] So about the other choices...
> Enough For Everyone (1) requires **6 enemies within 6m** of the player, meaning it is only active under specific circumstances. Rain of Lead (3) only adds +2 Multishot... which is nice but then again [[Galvanized Diffusion]] exists. Also, Total Damage is calculated based on **all** the pellets hitting the enemy, which is nearly impossible to achieve given the wide bullet spread.

Increase Base Critical Chance by +20%
