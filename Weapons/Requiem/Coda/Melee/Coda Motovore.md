I don't know what I did but I may have made my own Motovore weaker...? It should be hitting waaay harder.

## <DT_ IMPACT>

When modded for Impact damage, Coda Motovore gains +40% Attack Speed.

Valence Bonus: Heat
Arcane: Melee Animosity
